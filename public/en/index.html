01 · Services

Smart solutions. More value for your business.

Every engagement starts from a real process in your operation - not from a licence we need to sell. If it can be solved with what you already have, that is what we do.

01

Process automation (RPA)

Bots that run the routine the way a person runs it today: open the legacy system with no API, download the report, check it, post it, file it. With a log of every run and exception handling - whatever the bot cannot decide goes back to a human.

02

System integrations

ERP, CRM, e-commerce, bank, spreadsheet and third-party APIs talking to each other without someone in the middle typing it twice. With queueing, retries and failure alerts - an integration that breaks silently is worse than no integration.

03

Power Platform

Power Apps for the form that is a shared spreadsheet today, Power Automate for the approval flow, Power BI to stop building reports by hand. Inside the Microsoft 365 your company already subscribes to.

04

Internal platforms

A portal, an operations dashboard or a bespoke system, with corporate sign-in and access profiles - for when the process no longer fits in a low-code app.

05

Data and reporting

Consolidating the data that is scattered today, a data model and a dashboard that opens up to date - instead of a PDF someone assembles every Monday.

06

Process discovery

Before automating: map it, measure it and cut what should not exist. Automating a bad process only makes the mistake happen faster.

04 · How we work

From the first conversation to a flow in production

Step 1

Map

30 minutes on a call, at no cost. You show the process as it is today and we tell you whether it is worth automating.

Step 2

Design and quote

The proposed flow, the systems involved, the exceptions we expect, the timeline and the price. What is out of scope is written down too.

Step 3

Build and validate

Partial deliveries so the team can validate early. The people who run the process test it before it goes live.

Step 4

Deploy and hand over

Deployment in your environment, documentation, training and monitoring of every run.
